\begin{abstract}
Revision \texttt{v5.0} proved that \emph{Tabula Rasa} pretraining on $D_4$ lattice bases eliminates post-training quantization (PTQ) shock, yet identified two scaling bottlenecks: sequential Python recurrence over per-token Widrow-Hoff updates stalled GPU Tensor Cores, and single-matrix associative fast weights $\mathbf{W}_{\text{scar}} \in \mathbb{R}^{d_h \times d_h}$ could not simultaneously preserve verbatim high-entropy hashes alongside long-horizon semantic associations. In \textbf{Zenodo Revision \texttt{v6.0}}, we resolve both bottlenecks and extend Scar-Native pretraining to continuous 3D generative geometry across Native PyTorch and \textbf{$26 + 12$ pure \texttt{WebGPU WGSL 1.0} compute shaders}:
(1) \textbf{Parallel Chunkwise WY Widrow-Hoff Kernel (\texttt{chunkwise\_wy.py})}: reformulating sequential orthogonal eviction updates via the unit-lower-triangular WY representation $(\mathbf{I}_C + \mathbf{L}_{ab})^{-1}$ achieves a $\mathbf{12.63\times}$ forward+backward speedup on \texttt{NVIDIA A100-SXM4-40GB} ($93.30$~ms vs.\ $1{,}178.51$~ms at $B=24, T=512, C=64$) with $\cos_{\text{fwd}}=\mathbf{0.99999988}$ and $\cos_{\text{grad}}=\mathbf{0.99999982}$;
(2) \textbf{\texttt{ScarNativeLM v6.0} ($12$ Layers, $3$ Shared Stages, BPE-$4096$)}: combining \texttt{SharedStageUniversalBase}, per-layer identity scars, \texttt{Top-2 ReLU INT4} \texttt{ScarMoSExpertBank}, and \texttt{HierarchicalSCacheAttentionV6} ($4$ sinks $+ 20$ ring $+ 8$ surprise-promoted RoPE micro-pages $+ \mathbf{W}_{\text{scar}}$ in $\mathbf{1{,}329{,}408}$~\textbf{B}, $\Delta=0$~B) packs into $\mathbf{4.13}$~\textbf{MiB} ($15.00\times$ smaller than $62.02$~MiB FP16 Dense; $1.90$~MiB Stage-Streaming peak with $\Delta\text{logits}=\mathbf{0.0}$), achieves $\mathbf{0.000000}$~nats PTQ shock, improves held-out perplexity ($\mathrm{PPL}=\mathbf{1.3949}$ vs.\ $1.5829$ Dense), sustains $\mathbf{100.0\%}$ verbatim passkey recall up to $T=567$ ($28.4\times$ ring), and scales \texttt{v6-Frontier} ($96\text{L}, d=8192, 128\text{K}$ ctx) from $205.00$~GiB down to $\mathbf{4.65}$~\textbf{GiB} (\texttt{MoS-LRU}) or $\mathbf{0.71}$~\textbf{GiB} in Stage-Streaming;
(3) \textbf{Procrustes \texttt{mos-digest} + Dual Ridge Key + \texttt{<abstain>}}: compiling $4$ English Markdown documents into $24 \times 12$~KB ($3\times 4$~KB) RRR cartridges ($0$ trunk SGD) routes across \texttt{PT/EN/ES/FR} via $\mathbf{R}_\ell \in \mathrm{SO}(512)$ + dual Ridge gating at $\mathbf{98.96\%}$ ($95/96$), $0$ out-of-cone false triggers, $\mathbf{93.75\%}$ ($15/16$) unseen epistemic \texttt{<abstain>}, and $\mathbf{100.0\%}$ ($24/24$) reversion to \texttt{<abstain>} under document-level \texttt{0x00} GDPR erasure ($\Delta\text{logits}=0.0$), verified on $26$ Apple M2 WebGPU shaders ($\min\cos=\mathbf{0.99999988}$); and
(4) \textbf{\texttt{PointScarFlow v1.0}}: a $3.87$~MiB Tabula-Rasa \texttt{ScarNativeLinear} 3D Optimal-Transport Rectified Flow generator ($12$ WGSL shaders, $4$ Euler steps) conditioned on \texttt{Qwen2.5-3B} Layer-$23$ $P_{\perp\text{lang}}$ reduces held-out Chamfer-$L_2$ by $\mathbf{7.51\times}$ ($0.0621$ vs.\ $0.4663$ sphere) with $0.9974$--$0.9978$ cross-lingual 3D shape cosine across \texttt{PT/EN/ES/FR}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction: Scaling Tabula-Rasa Scar-Native Systems}
Autoregressive Transformers~\citep{vaswani2017attention} across servers, laptops, and client-side WebGPU browsers~\citep{w3c2024webgpu}---as well as continuous 3D generative models~\citep{lipman2023flow,liu2023flow} and physical high-dynamic-range sensor arrays~\citep{leshem2000multichannel}---face severe memory and structural bottlenecks when built on unconstrained dense matrices ($135.43$~GiB at $72.71\text{B}$~\citep{dettmers2022llmint8,lin2024awq}; $205.00$~GiB at $96\text{L}, d=8192$ with $128\text{K}$ KV cache). While Revision~\texttt{v5.0} abolished the \emph{Pretrained Gaussian Backbone Floor} on a $4$-layer prototype, scaling \emph{Tabula Rasa} Scar-Native pretraining exposed four engineering and mathematical walls:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Sequential Fast-Weight Recurrence Bottleneck:} Per-token Python/CUDA loops for orthogonal Widrow-Hoff eviction~\citep{widrow1960adaptive,schmidhuber1992learning,schlag2021linear} underutilize Tensor Cores ($1{,}178.51$~ms/step at $T=512$).
    \item \textbf{Verbatim vs.\ Associative $\mathcal{O}(1)$ Memory Tension:} Sliding windows~\citep{xiao2024efficient,zhang2023h2o} suffer total amnesia beyond $W_{\text{win}}$, whereas smooth associative matrices $\mathbf{W}_{\text{scar}} \in \mathbb{R}^{d_h \times d_h}$~\citep{yang2024parallelizing} blur exact multi-digit passkeys at deep horizons.
    \item \textbf{Cross-Layer Footprint, Polyglot Corpus Digestion \& Post-GDPR Neighbor Bleed:} Storing independent universal bases in every layer scales linearly with depth $L$, while single-cone cosine routing~\citep{meng2022locating,fang2025alphaedit,dettmers2023qlora} risks falling back to same-template neighbor cartridges when a document is wiped via \texttt{0x00} GDPR erasure unless paired with discriminative dual-key gating and native epistemic abstention (\texttt{<abstain>}).
    \item \textbf{Cross-Modal 3D Generative Geometry in the Browser:} Synthesizing continuous 3D point manifolds ($N=1{,}024$ points + normals) from multilingual LLM hidden states without discrete mesh catalogs or heavy diffusion loops requires straight Optimal-Transport flows~\citep{lipman2023flow,liu2023flow} compiled natively on \texttt{VQ4-Scar} lattices.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Mathematical \& Systems Innovations in Revision v6.0}

\subsection{Chunkwise WY Parallel Widrow-Hoff Scan (\texttt{chunkwise\_wy.py})}
Let token $e$ evicted from the local window carry unit Pre-RoPE key $\hat{\mathbf{k}}_e \in \mathbb{S}^{d_h - 1}$, value $\mathbf{v}_e \in \mathbb{R}^{d_h}$, decay $\gamma_e = 1 - \lambda_h g_e$, and write vector $\mathbf{a}_e = g_e \eta_h \hat{\mathbf{k}}_e$. Grouping $C=64$ steps into a chunk~\citep{bischof1987wy,yang2024parallelizing}, the sequential rank-$1$ transition $\mathbf{W}_{t} = \gamma_t \mathbf{W}_{t-1}(\mathbf{I} - \mathbf{a}_t \hat{\mathbf{k}}_t^\top) + \mathbf{v}_t \mathbf{a}_t^\top$ collapses into parallel Tensor-Core GEMMs via the unit-lower-triangular WY resolver:
\begin{equation}
    \big(\mathbf{I}_C + \mathbf{L}_{ab}\big)\mathbf{U}_{\text{eff}} = \widetilde{\mathbf{V}}, \qquad \big(\mathbf{I}_C + \mathbf{L}_{ab}\big)\mathbf{K}_{\text{eff}} = \widetilde{\mathbf{K}},
    \label{eq:chunkwise_wy}
\end{equation}
where $(\mathbf{L}_{ab})_{i,j} = (\prod_{m=j+1}^{i}\gamma_m)(\mathbf{a}_i^\top \hat{\mathbf{k}}_j)$ for $i > j$. Solving the $64\times 64$ triangular system once per chunk yields exact state transitions and intra-chunk reads at $\mathbf{12.63\times}$ higher training speed on \texttt{A100} ($\cos_{\text{fwd}}=0.99999988$, $\cos_{\text{grad}}=0.99999982$).

\subsection{Shared-Stage \texttt{MoS}, Dual-Tier \texttt{SCache} $\mathcal{O}(1)$ \& Stage-Streaming}
Instead of duplicating dense matrices across $L$ layers, \texttt{ScarNativeLM v6.0} partitions $L=12$ layers into $S=3$ stages ($4$ layers/stage). Each stage holds a single \texttt{SharedStageUniversalBase} ($\widehat{\mathbf{U}}_{\text{VQ4}}^{(s)}$ on $D_4$ \texttt{LUT256} + $\mathbf{d}_{\text{eq}}^{(s)}$ + \texttt{FWHT-64}), while layer $\ell \in s$ adds a rank-$16$ identity scar $(\mathbf{A}_{\text{id}}^{(\ell)}, \mathbf{B}_{\text{id}}^{(\ell)})$ plus a \texttt{Top-2 ReLU INT4} \texttt{ScarMoSExpertBank} ($E=4$ experts, WGSL Shaders~\#23--\#24):
\begin{equation}
    \mathbf{y}^{(\ell)}(\mathbf{x}) = \mathbf{y}_{\text{stage}}^{(s)}(\mathbf{x}) + \mathbf{x}\,Q_4(\mathbf{A}_{\text{id}}^{(\ell)})^\top Q_4(\mathbf{B}_{\text{id}}^{(\ell)})^\top + \sum_{m \in \mathcal{T}_2(\mathbf{x})} w_m(\mathbf{x})\,\mathbf{x}\,Q_4(\mathbf{A}_m^{(\ell)})^\top Q_4(\mathbf{B}_m^{(\ell)})^\top.
    \label{eq:shared_stage_mos}
\end{equation}
To unite verbatim copying with unbounded semantic horizon in $\Delta B/\Delta T = 0$, \texttt{HierarchicalSCacheAttentionV6} (Shaders~\#25--\#26) pairs Tier~1 ($4$ RoPE sinks $+ 20$ sliding ring $+ M_{\text{micro}}=8$ surprise-promoted RoPE micro-pages storing exact $(\mathbf{k}_{\text{rope}}, \mathbf{v})$ whenever prediction surprise exceeds $\theta_{\text{micro}}=0.35$) with Tier~2 (evicting displaced micro-pages pre-RoPE into $\mathbf{W}_{\text{scar}}^{(h)}$). Auxiliary micro-page dropout ($p=0.5$) during pretraining prevents shortcutting and trains both tiers jointly.

\begin{theorem}[Stage-Streaming Invariance, Dual-Tier $\mathcal{O}(1)$ Bound \& Dual-Key GDPR Reversion]
\label{thm:v6}
In \texttt{ScarNativeLM v6.0}: (i) loading one \texttt{SharedStageUniversalBase} at a time into GPU VRAM (\emph{Sequential Stage-Streaming}, $1.90$~MiB weight buffer for $12\text{L}$; $0.71$~GiB total peak for $96\text{L}$ \texttt{v6-Frontier}) produces bit-identical logits ($\max|\Delta\text{logits}| = 0.0$) to full-resident execution; (ii) \texttt{HierarchicalSCacheAttentionV6} occupies strictly constant $1{,}329{,}408$~B ($\Delta B / \Delta T = 0$ for all $T \ge 1$) while preserving exact RoPE attention over surprise-promoted tokens; and (iii) gating each $12{,}288$~B ($3\times 4{,}096$~B) \texttt{mos-digest} cartridge by both Procrustes-aligned~\citep{schonemann1966generalized} spherical-cap cosine $\cos(\mathbf{z}_{\text{probe}}, \mathbf{z}_{\text{anchor}}) > \tau_{\text{cos}}$ and discriminative English Ridge dual score $\mathbf{z}_{\text{probe}}^\top \mathbf{z}_{\text{dual}} > \tau_{\text{dual}}$ guarantees zero neighbor fallback upon \texttt{0x00} document erasure ($100\%$ reversion to \texttt{<abstain>}, $\Delta\text{logits}=0.0$).
\end{theorem}

\subsection{Procrustes \texttt{mos-digest} \& Continuous 3D \texttt{PointScarFlow v1.0}}
At Golden-Slice Layer $L^\star=9$, \texttt{ProcrustesLanguageNullspace} solves orthogonal Procrustes~\citep{schonemann1966generalized} $\mathbf{R}_\ell = \arg\min_{\mathbf{R} \in \mathrm{SO}(d)} \|\mathbf{H}_\ell \mathbf{R} - \mathbf{H}_{\text{EN}}\|_F$ for $\ell \in \{\text{PT},\text{ES},\text{FR}\}$ and projects residual syntax via $\mathbf{P}_{\perp\text{lang}}$ ($k_{\text{lang}}=16$). During inference, $\mathbf{R}_\ell$ folds orthogonally into $Q_4(\mathbf{A}_{\text{scar}})$ at zero extra token FLOPs. Simultaneously, \textbf{\texttt{PointScarFlow v1.0}} (\texttt{scar\_native/point\_scar\_flow.py}, $11.85\text{M}$ parameters in \texttt{ScarNativeLinear}, zero \texttt{nn.Linear}, $12$ WGSL shaders) learns an Optimal-Transport $W_2^2$-paired Rectified Flow~\citep{lipman2023flow,liu2023flow} velocity field $\mathbf{v}_\theta(\mathbf{X}_\tau, \tau, \mathbf{P}_{\perp\text{lang}}\bar{\mathbf{h}}_{23})$ over $N=1{,}024$ 3D points + surface normals, self-distilled into $4$ deterministic Euler steps ($3.87$~MiB WebGPU shard).
