## 3. Architectural Scope & Anti-Facade Guarantees

1. **Physical Pointer Auditing (`0.00 B` Discrepancy):**
   - `recursive_scache_storage_bytes(cache)` (in [`speculative_ghost_jump.py`](../scar_cache/speculative_ghost_jump.py)), `audit_unique_module_bytes(module)` (in [`sinkhorn_rebasin.py`](../scar_cache/sinkhorn_rebasin.py)), and `unique_tensor_storage_bytes()` (in [`scar_codec/core.py`](../scar_codec/core.py)) walk the entire Python/PyTorch object graph via `tensor.untyped_storage().data_ptr()` to prove that no hidden $\mathcal{O}(T)$ buffers or duplicate weight copies exist behind reported metrics.
2. **Zero Dense `nn.Linear` Inside `ScarNativeLM v5.0`:**
   - `test_scar_native_lm_has_zero_nn_linear_and_exact_o1_stepping` verifies `not any(isinstance(m, nn.Linear) for m in model.modules())` and confirms that `model.pack_all_to_scar_binary()` drops `100%` of master floats (`w_master = None`, `embed_tokens = None`) with `0.000000` KL export shock.
3. **Genuine Bit-Packed Tensors (Zero Float Masks):**
   - `CanonicalMoSGroup` & `ProtectedInt4ScarMatrix` (`INT4`) pack 2 weights per `torch.uint8` byte (`packed_base.numel() * 2 == N * K`).
   - `VQ4ScarMatrix`, `NativeVQ4ScarPackedMatrix` & `PhysicalVQ4ScarPackage` pack 4D lattice vector codes at `1 uint8` per 4 scalars (`codes_u32.numel() * 16 == N * K`) with `LUT256[0] = (0,0,0,0)` exact vacuum invariance.
   - `Sub2BitMoSLinear` (`2.25 bpw`) packs 4 quaternary codes per `torch.uint8` byte (`packed_2bit.numel() * 4 == N * K`).
   - `DetachedScarStore` (`8,192 B = 2 × 4,096 B`), `HeadExpandedScarStore`, & `GoldenSliceScarStoreV4` (`143,360 B = 35 × 4,096 B`) write raw OS-page-aligned binary slots supporting microsecond `0x00` GDPR unlearning.
4. **Clear Distinction Between `ScarNativeLM` (*Tabula-Rasa* Foundation Engine), `CanonicalMoSEngine` (`72.71B` Real Pretrained Weights), and `NativeMoS70B` (Recurrent Depth Expansion):**
   - [`scar_native/model.py`](../scar_native/model.py) (`ScarNativeLM`) is pretrained **100% from scratch (`step = 0` random initialization)** without dense layers.
   - [`scar_cache/cli_72b.py`](../scar_cache/cli_72b.py) (`CanonicalMoSEngine`) executes the **real `80`-layer `Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct` (`72.71B`)** pretrained weights across `20` SHA-256 verified shards.
